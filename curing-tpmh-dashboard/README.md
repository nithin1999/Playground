## Latest improvement — Response-Time Service-Level Curve
Alarm concurrency and saturation show demand pressure, but neither proves whether a 2-operator design responds quickly enough. Thursday should therefore measure the complete response-time distribution for every meaningful abnormal call: event/call timestamp | owner becomes aware | response starts | arrival at press/work point | containment/first effective action | clear | route restored | PRIMARY/FLEX/support source.

Do not invent a response-time pass threshold. Compare the 2-operator response distribution with existing plant requirements where they exist and with matched historical staffed-shift performance. Report median, 90th percentile, maximum, and the share exceeding any real plant-defined requirement. Stratify by zone, PRIMARY/FLEX ownership, break state, saturation state and demand state.

Interpretation: slow response concentrated in distant zones = route/layout problem; slow response during breaks = relief problem; slow response when FLEX is qualified but ownership transfer is delayed = dispatch/handoff problem; slow response while both operators are already committed = capacity/surge evidence; good average response with a long upper tail = fragile abnormal-response performance that TPMH alone can hide.

## Risks / gaps
- No verified Thursday 2-operator performance exists yet.
- Exact plant-authorized containment/restart and response-time requirements have not been supplied here.
- Alarm concurrency is a demand proxy, not operator work interference.
- Shift-average TPMH can hide slow abnormal response, tail-risk events, late-shift deterioration and break fragility.
- Break relief can falsely validate staffing if it shifts labor/backlog into another role.
- Hidden support, excessive cross-zone FLEX and donor-role displacement can falsely validate 2-operator staffing.
- No saturation on a low-demand Thursday is under-exposure, not proof.
- A single shift can leave important demand states NOT TESTED.
- Prior idle observations are not removable labor until base work, walking, machine-auto time and abnormal reserve are separated.
