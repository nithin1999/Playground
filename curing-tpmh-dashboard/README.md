## Latest improvement — Pilot Integrity Exposure States
Do not judge Thursday as one binary pass/fail shift. Classify elapsed pilot time by the staffing condition actually being tested. This prevents a nominal 2-operator shift from looking successful when unplanned third-person labor, coaching, or containment temporarily changes the experiment.

### Exposure states
- **INDEPENDENT 2-OP:** Op1 + Op2 perform the frozen method with normal baseline support only; no staffing-substitution help.
- **ASSISTED 2-OP:** the two operators remain primary, but incremental third-person coaching, fetching, coverage, prioritization or hands-on help occurs. Log role, reason, start/end and people-minutes.
- **CONTAINED / NOT TESTABLE:** the frozen staffing concept is temporarily no longer being tested because plant safety/quality/process authority, major special cause, or rescue action requires a different operating condition.

Every transition must be timestamped. Do not discard assisted or contained periods; analyze why they occurred. Do not invent a required percentage of independent time before Thursday.

### Pilot Integrity calculation
`Independent Exposure % = Independent 2-op minutes / observable pilot minutes × 100`

Report this with Scheduled TPMH, Effective Labor / Adjusted TPMH, support people-minutes, breaks, protected-work completion, abnormalities, delayed work and recovery. A high Independent Exposure % is not sufficient by itself if safety, quality, delivery, break coverage or protected work fails.

## Risks / gaps
- Operators may behave differently because they know the study is occurring; leader behavior must be consistent and non-punitive.
- A qualified third person can unintentionally make a weak design look successful through reminders, fetching, prioritizing or brief unlogged help.
- Misclassifying normal baseline support as incremental help can unfairly penalize 2-op; establish the distinction before analysis.
- Thursday may be an unusually easy or difficult production condition; retain the Exposure Signature.
- Break coverage can export work to Mold/Bladder/Team Lead; record resulting home-role delay/recovery rather than treating relief as free.
- Fixed zones can create local overload; do not improvise frequent zone switching unless the frozen abnormal rule calls for it.
- Tricycle rejection means residual walking remains part of the staffing design.
