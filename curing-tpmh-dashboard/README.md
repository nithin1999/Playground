## Latest improvement — Pre-Registered Pilot Decision Rules
Before Thursday, freeze how the evidence will be interpreted so the conclusion cannot drift toward whichever metric looks favorable after the shift. The pilot is evaluated across Safety/Quality, Delivery/TPMH, workload/queue, break recovery, abnormal-response exposure, qualification coverage, and outside-support dependence.

### Decision ladder
- **STOP / CONTAIN:** Any existing safety, quality, equipment-protection, authorization or plant stop rule is triggered. Protect the process first; log the event. No productivity target overrides this.
- **NOT VALIDATED:** Material pilot-only outside support masks the two-operator model; protected/due work remains unresolved; recovery debt persists at shift end; or critical operating states are NOT TESTED. A favorable TPMH does not override these gaps.
- **PROMISING — RETEST:** Two operators complete the observed in-standard exposure without unresolved protected work and without material pilot-only support, but exposure coverage is incomplete, comparator quality is weak, or workload-model uncertainty remains material.
- **SUPPORTED FOR REPRESENTATIVE RETEST:** Observed in-standard operation maintains required safety/quality controls, required work and break recovery; abnormal events are handled under the frozen response rules; support dependence is within the defined normal model; and exposure is sufficiently representative to justify another controlled run. One shift alone is not treated as proof of permanent staffing sustainability.

### Pre-registration rule
Before seeing Thursday's results, write down the required measures, definitions, exposure states, normal-model support boundary and decision ladder. Preserve raw results even when unfavorable. Do not invent numerical limits for response time, queue age, support, or workload where plant requirements have not been verified; classify those requirements as NOT VERIFIED.
