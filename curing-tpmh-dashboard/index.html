Latest improvement — Support Neutrality Rule

Classify every non-Op1/Op2 intervention by why the help was needed, not merely by the helper's job title. This prevents normal baseline support from unfairly penalizing 2-op and prevents extra staffing-substitution help from making 2-op look independent.

BASELINE-NORMAL: support that would ordinarily occur under the existing 3-op method for the same condition.
INCREMENTAL-2OP: help required because the proposed 2-op system could not independently absorb the work. Charge people-minutes to Effective Labor and classify the period ASSISTED.
AMBIGUOUS: causality cannot be established. Preserve separately; do not force a conclusion.
CONTAINMENT/RESCUE: plant safety/quality/process authority requires intervention; affected exposure is CONTAINED / NOT TESTABLE.

Freeze these definitions before Thursday. If the baseline is unclear, use AMBIGUOUS rather than post-hoc judgment.
