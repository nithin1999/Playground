Latest improvement — Two-Operator Saturation Exposure

Saturation episode: Op1 and Op2 are both committed to required work and additional required work becomes ready but cannot immediately be owned without delaying, interrupting or transferring existing work.

Log start, both operators' current work, triggering demand, pending work/oldest wait, support used and clear time. This measures observed operator-demand interference rather than assuming that simultaneous alarms equal overload.

After the pilot review episodes/hour, total saturation minutes, peak pending work, oldest wait, recovery debt generated, and whether each episode cleared independently or required support. No invented pass/fail threshold.

How to interpret saturation

Break concentration → relief design. Task-specific concentration → qualification/standard work. Long-walk concentration → motion/layout. Rare abnormal clusters only → surge-response design. Repeated saturation under representative ordinary demand → direct capacity/design concern. No saturation on a quiet shift is NOT proof of adequate staffing.
