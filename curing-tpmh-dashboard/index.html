Latest improvement — Break-Relief Capacity Protection

A 2-operator design is not full-shift feasible unless breaks can occur without hidden staffing substitution or unrecovered backlog. Treat each break as a capacity transition.

COVERED: qualified relief assumes the frozen responsibility and required work remains controlled.
DEGRADED: relief occurs but work is deferred, the relief person's home role accumulates work, or ownership becomes unclear.
UNRELIEVED / CONTAINED: planned relief cannot be provided or escalation is required.

Relief debt = required Curing or relief-home-role work displaced during relief that remains incomplete at handback. Track it until recovery, including extra people-minutes.

Thursday observation

Continuous: abnormalities, flex assists, third-person help, exposure-state changes, break/relief transitions, delayed work, containment and recovery.

Fixed 15 min/hour: work category, walking/retrieval, machine interaction, zone adherence, overlapping demand, relief state and carryover work. Keep windows fixed.

Pilot integrity

INDEPENDENT 2-OP = frozen method + normal baseline support. ASSISTED 2-OP = incremental staffing substitution. CONTAINED = concept temporarily not testable.

Independent Exposure % = Independent 2-op minutes / observable pilot minutes × 100

Best current strategy

  1. Freeze roles, zones, qualifications and relief.
  2. Scenario-walk simultaneous demand, abnormality during break, relief handback, flex assist and a two-person task.
  3. Thursday: continuous exception log + fixed 15 min/hour observation.
  4. Classify each break COVERED / DEGRADED / UNRELIEVED and track relief debt to recovery.
  5. Calculate Scheduled TPMH + Adjusted TPMH + Independent Exposure % + incremental support + relief-debt recovery.
  6. Separate design failure from execution failure; Pareto causes.
  7. Redesign and repeat representative conditions before permanent 2-op.
