Latest improvement — Demonstrated Skill-Readiness Gate

A qualification label does not automatically prove usable independent capacity. Before someone is counted as Op1/Op2, break-relief or abnormal-response capacity, verify that the required work has been demonstrated under representative conditions.

Evidence ladder: Not trained → Trained/observed → Demonstrated with coaching → Demonstrated independently.
Capacity rule: only independently demonstrated capability is counted as independent coverage. Coached performance is training evidence and the coaching/support must remain visible.
Critical work: use a TWI-style Job Breakdown — important steps, key points and reasons why — followed by try-out/teach-back and independent demonstration.
Governance: plant authorization plus safety/quality requirements always override this project classification.
