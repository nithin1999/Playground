Diagnostic

Qualified Load Ratio = qualified hands-on demand minutes / qualified operator-minutes available. A ratio above 1.00 means observed qualified demand exceeded locally available qualified labor in that interval. It is an arithmetic diagnostic, not a new plant response threshold. Keep machine automatic time separate and never convert alarm duration directly into operator labor.

Why this matters

A shift can average 55.8% utilization and still fail during breaks, simultaneous abnormalities or skill-specific demand. Conversely, total minutes can look overloaded while machine automatic time requires no operator attention. Interval reconstruction identifies whether the real constraint is workload, qualification, motion, concurrency, relief or outside-support dependence.
