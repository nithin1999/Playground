Latest improvement — relief capacity reservation

Break relief is now treated as a time-phased capacity condition, not an informal promise that another person can help. A qualified Mold/Bladder/Team Lead does not count as available relief if covering Curing causes their own due work to accumulate.

Reserve: primary + alternate qualified relief for every break/lunch window and crew.
Retain: the relief person's own protected workload in the capacity calculation.
Observe: actual break start/end, helper minutes, displaced helper work and carryover.
Judge the system: successful production alone is insufficient if relief creates hidden support, protected-work failure, backlog or overload elsewhere.

No arbitrary utilization or missed-break threshold is assumed. Use plant requirements where defined and compare identical measures in 3-op baseline versus shadow 2-op.
