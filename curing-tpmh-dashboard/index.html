Latest improvement — Pre-Pilot Scenario Challenge / Dry-Run Gate

Before live exposure, walk the proposed two-operator standard at the gemba. This is a design-verification gate, not proof of capacity. The objective is to expose ambiguous ownership, qualification gaps, impossible routes, break conflicts and shared-resource dependencies before they contaminate a live pilot.

Challenge normal + abnormal interaction: alarm during routine work; simultaneous cross-zone alarms; protected work becoming due while an operator is occupied.
Challenge staffing: active abnormality during break relief; one operator unavailable; known two-person intervention.
Challenge shared resources: gantry/manual-support condition; leader or outside support requested.
For every scenario: identify owner, qualification, route, protected-work decision, queue action, escalation/containment and outside labor.
Result: PASS / GAP / NOT YET TESTABLE. A dry-run pass only means the standard is explicit enough to test; it does not prove 2-op feasibility.

Best current strategy

  1. Remove avoidable motion and point-of-use gaps.
  2. Demand-weight Op1/Op2 zones by work + travel + abnormal exposure.
  3. Build fixed-window Variability-Aware Reserve distributions.
  4. Test Cross-Zone Collision exposure and shared-resource conflicts.
  5. Apply Qualification Redundancy / Pair-Robustness Gate.
  6. Freeze NORMAL routes and ABNORMAL single-owner dispatch.
  7. Protect due work with Protected Work Matrix + recovery queue.
  8. Close critical single-point skill gaps.
  9. Use formal break handoffs and retain relief home-role demand.
  10. Use Pilot Leader Standard Work without hidden third-operator labor.
  11. Run Pre-Pilot Scenario Challenge and close design ambiguities.
  12. Freeze measurement, escalation and containment/restart rules.
  13. Collect matched 3-op baseline.
  14. Shadow 2-op; measure queue, support, breaks, collisions and adherence.
  15. Calculate scheduled TPMH, Effective Labor and Adjusted TPMH.
  16. Pareto exceptions and redesign.
  17. Expand across demand, downtime, late-shift and A/B/C/D conditions before true 2-op.
