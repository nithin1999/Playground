Latest improvement — pilot escalation and recovery control

Before live 2-operator exposure, define what happens when the staffing condition begins to lose control. Safe support must always be available, but any extra help must be visible in the analysis rather than making the trial appear self-sufficient.

GREEN: continue the defined 2-op standard.
AMBER: cross-support/escalate under the defined response rule and log any intervention.
RED: suspend the 2-op exposure and restore the required support until the plant-defined recovery condition is met.

Use existing YTMM safety, quality and process requirements where hard limits exist. Do not invent numeric response-time, backlog, utilization or downtime limits. Minimum trigger families are safety/quality abnormalities, both operators occupied, qualified coverage unavailable, protected work approaching a required deadline, relief unavailable, support-area carryover, major equipment abnormality, and supervisor-directed escalation.
