Latest improvement — Relief Coverage Feasibility Matrix

A healthy pooled capacity number does not prove that the right qualified capacity exists at the right time. Before shadow 2-op, explicitly test who can own each critical normal, protected and abnormal task while Op1 or Op2 is on break, committed to an abnormality, or otherwise unavailable.

Matrix: critical task × Op1 × Op2 × relief source × leader/shadow × shared support. Record demonstrated qualification/authorization and restrictions.
Time-phase it: overlay planned break windows and abnormal-response scenarios. Identify any required task with no qualified owner or only a fragile single-person dependency.
No hidden labor: record the relief person's displaced home-role work and recovery people-minutes in Effective Labor.
Rule: do not invent a numerical redundancy target. Plant safety/quality/authorization rules and demonstrated qualification govern coverage.
