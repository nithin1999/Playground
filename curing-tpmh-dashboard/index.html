Latest improvement — Break-Relief Capacity Envelope

Breaks and meals are now treated as a planned operating state. The 2-operator design must show how required work is maintained while one operator is off-floor without hiding incremental third-person capacity or backlog inside shift-average TPMH.

Capture each break: start/end · operator off floor · relief person/qualification · running presses · abnormal events · transferred work · backlog start/peak/handback · incremental support people-minutes · normal-route restoration.

StateMeaning
INDEPENDENTDefined qualified capacity maintains required work without incremental 2-op support.
RECOVEREDTemporary backlog clears under the defined relief design within plant requirements.
ASSISTEDIncremental capacity outside the defined relief design is required.
CONTAINEDNormal plant rescue/containment is invoked.
NOT-EXPOSEDNo meaningful break-state exposure occurred.

Relief debt: work displaced by a break stays visible until completed. Repeated carryover after handback signals a relief, ownership, skill or route-balance problem even when shift TPMH is acceptable.

Abnormal Response Ladder

NORMAL ROUTE → ABNORMALITY VISIBLE/CALLED → PRIMARY OWNER → FLEX OWNER → FLOOR LEADER → CONTAINMENT/RESCUE → BACKLOG RECOVERY → NORMAL ROUTE

Use existing plant-authorized rules; do not invent time thresholds.

Hour-by-Hour Demand-to-Capacity Board

Capture plan/actual, running presses, abnormal events, Op1/Op2 direct work, walk/retrieval, queues, oldest backlog, relief/support, independent exposure and dominant cause.

Best current strategy

  1. Complete readiness gates and freeze people, routes, skills, relief and plant-authorized reaction rules.
  2. Freeze break/meal coverage: who leaves, who covers, transferred work, qualifications, handoff and handback.
  3. Scenario-walk a break plus abnormality before the pilot.
  4. Run Thursday with exception logging, break-window records, reaction timestamps, exposure tracking and hourly demand/capacity control.
  5. Reconstruct occupancy, queues, backlog, relief debt, support and abnormal-response paths.
  6. Normalize against running presses, events, mix and downtime.
  7. Diagnose degraded periods by capacity, skill, motion/routing, relief, recovery, equipment/process or support dependency.
  8. Change only the demonstrated constraint and repeat representative conditions before permanent staffing change.
