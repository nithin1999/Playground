Latest improvement — third-person dependency rate

A 2-operator shift must not be called successful merely because production is achieved while Team Lead, Mold, Bladder, Gantry, supervisor, maintenance or another operator repeatedly absorbs Curing work. The pilot now separates normal-required support from staffing-dependent support.

Every intervention: helper role, start/end, task/reason, normal-required vs staffing-dependent, helper displaced work, and alarm/event ID when applicable.
Normalize exposure: staffing-dependent intervention minutes per 100 running press-hours; alarm-driven staffing interventions per 100 alarm events.
Compare fairly: collect the same measures in the 3-op baseline and shadow 2-op under characterized operating context.

No arbitrary acceptance threshold is assigned. The baseline and shadow evidence must establish the operating difference first. This control does not claim that hidden support currently exists.

Best current strategy

  1. Map presses; balance zones by occupied workload, peaks and travel.
  2. Standardize predictable work and define protected timing/priority.
  3. Use signal-driven abnormalities with explicit ownership/cross-support/escalation.
  4. Verify two-deep critical-task coverage across crews and relief windows.
  5. Prove break relief and time-phased capacity margin.
  6. Freeze measurement definitions, Leader Standard Work, SIC and intervention taxonomy.
  7. Collect context-characterized 3-op baseline with identical dependency/backlog measures.
  8. Shadow 2-op: measure concurrent demand, staffing-dependent support, displaced work and recovery aging.
  9. Pareto overload/support/backlog causes → revise → sustained A/B/C/D rotation → true 2-op only after gates pass.
