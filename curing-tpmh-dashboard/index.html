Latest improvement — Route Burden & Point-of-Use Motion Gate

Before locking Op1/Op2 zones, map actual normal and abnormal-response paths and convert travel into operator-minutes using observed trip frequency. Do not balance zones by press count alone.

MAP: trace actual routes for checks, alarms, GIP/scan, manual work, relief and retrieval.
WEIGHT: route burden = observed travel time × observed trip frequency; short frequent trips can dominate.
CLASSIFY: separate required process travel from retrieval/search, information checking, relief travel and avoidable backtracking.
REMOVE: test point-of-use tools/materials, visual status, staging and route sequence before rebalance.
REBALANCE: weight zones by remaining manual work + travel + abnormal exposure.
VERIFY: only measured reductions count as capacity improvement; carry residual travel into the capacity envelope.

Best current strategy

  1. Map actual recurring and abnormal-response routes; quantify frequency and travel time.
  2. Remove avoidable motion/retrieval/search and verify savings at gemba.
  3. Demand-weight Op1/Op2 zones by remaining work + travel + abnormal exposure.
  4. Build individual capacity envelopes and collision reserve.
  5. Prove skill readiness and time-phased qualified relief.
  6. Freeze normal routes, protected-work priorities and workload states.
  7. Use signal → acknowledge → arrival → hands-on → containment → recovery timestamps.
  8. Use a visible abnormality queue with approved criticality, FIFO within equal priority, owner, age and escalation.
  9. Account for relief debt and home-role recovery labor.
  10. Collect context-characterized 3-op baseline and Exposure Signatures.
  11. Freeze GO / HOLD / STOP criteria and shadow rescue rules.
  12. Run shadow 2-op with rescue-only third operator; log every intervention and dispatch decision.
  13. Compare exposure-matched windows using TPMH, Effective Labor, Support Burden, Adjusted TPMH, route burden and response/queue distributions.
  14. Pareto exceptions, redesign and repeat across representative A/B/C/D conditions before true 2-op.

Next action

Run a Route Burden Study during the next representative 3-op baseline before freezing zones. On the press-area layout trace actual paths and log operator/role, origin, destination, purpose/trigger, travel seconds, retrieval/search seconds, hands-on seconds and whether the trip was required or potentially avoidable. Summarize trips/shift × median travel time, attack the largest avoidable burdens, then remeasure before crediting savings.
