Latest improvement — Hour-by-Hour Demand-to-Capacity Board

Do not judge Thursday from shift TPMH alone. For each hour tie production demand to operator loading, abnormalities, backlog, motion and outside support.

CaptureWhy
Plan / actual / running pressesSeparates production demand and equipment state from staffing effects
Abnormal eventsShows event-driven demand intensity
Op1 / Op2 direct-work minutesTests whether both operators were actually committed
Walk / retrieval minutesExposes motion/layout opportunity before adding labor
Queues / oldest backlogShows whether demand temporarily exceeded response capacity
Relief/support minutesPrevents hidden third-person capacity from being credited to 2-op
Independent 2-op minutesShows clean exposure

Hourly state: GREEN = independent and required work current; YELLOW = temporary backlog/flex/support but recovered; RED = containment/rescue, unresolved required backlog, or applicable plant limit reached. Use real plant requirements—do not invent numerical limits.

How to diagnose a bad hour

Output down + operator capacity visible: investigate equipment/process loss. Both operators committed + queue/backlog rising: true capacity-collision evidence. Walking rises first: attack route/layout. Break-linked yellow/red: redesign relief. Abnormal-event-linked deterioration: strengthen abnormal-response standard/capacity.

Exposure Coverage Matrix

Classify critical states OBSERVED-INDEPENDENT / OBSERVED-ASSISTED / CONTAINED / NOT-EXPOSED. NOT-EXPOSED is neither pass nor fail and remains a validation gap.

Pre-Pilot Readiness

Use GO / GO-WITH-CONTAINMENT / NOT-READY. Freeze standard work, qualified capacity, abnormal response, relief and measurement. Observer records without coaching; normal leadership and rescue remain separate.
