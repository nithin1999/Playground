Why this matters

A RED event does not automatically prove two operators are impossible. Classify whether the cause is qualification, simultaneous demand, two-person work, route/dispatch, relief, equipment condition or true capacity, then correct and retest. Conversely, target TPMH achieved with unrecorded rescue labor or delayed protected work is not a valid staffing success.
