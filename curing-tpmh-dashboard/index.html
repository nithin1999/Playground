Best current strategy

  1. Freeze people, routes, skills, relief and reaction rules.
  2. Define the floor-leader/flex role and protected leader responsibilities.
  3. Freeze break/meal, protected-work ownership and historical matching method.
  4. Scenario-walk breaks, simultaneous demand, abnormalities and two-person tasks.
  5. Run Thursday with exception logging, reaction timestamps, exposure tracking and hourly plan-vs-actual.
  6. Capture 15-minute workload, standard-work deviations and event-level work collisions.
  7. Capture every leader/flex assist: reason, people-minutes, backlog effect and donor-role displacement.
  8. Reconstruct occupancy, queues, backlog, relief debt, support chains and recovery.
  9. Build collision, leader-reserve-demand and support-displacement Paretos.
  10. Normalize for running press-hours, events, mix and downtime; compare matched shifts.
  11. Separate persistent capacity from surge reserve, motion, skill, relief, equipment, standard practicality and staffing substitution.
  12. Change only the demonstrated constraint; repeat representative exposure before permanent staffing change.
