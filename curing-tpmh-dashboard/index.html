Pilot HOLD / Containment

Plant Safety, Quality and equipment-protection requirements override the experiment. If independent 2-op exposure is lost through staffing substitution, unrecoverable protected work, growing queue, failed break design or impractical frozen standard work: HOLD → contain/escalate → timestamp → preserve evidence → classify → authorized restart.

Leader Reserve + Support Displacement

Log every leader/flex/support assist with reason, people-minutes, backlog effect, donor-role displacement and restoration. Classify planned support, surge capacity, staffing substitution or containment.

Work-Interference / Collision Matrix

Log new demand arriving while operators are already committed. Rank combinations by backlog/delay, recovery debt, support people-minutes and failure to restore normal route—not frequency alone.

  11. Fix only the demonstrated constraint and repeat representative exposure before permanent staffing change.
