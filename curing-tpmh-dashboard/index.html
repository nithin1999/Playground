Latest improvement — Controlled Flex-Assist

Keep clear primary Op1/Op2 zones, but allow an explicit task transfer when uneven abnormal demand threatens due/protected work. This tests whether two operators can share capacity without turning the floor into random cross-zone chasing.

CALL — home-zone owner identifies need.
CHECK — assisting operator is qualified and has no higher-priority/protected work.
TRANSFER — one specific task/event is explicitly accepted; ownership remains clear.
WORK + LOG — record start/end, reason, travel and displaced home-zone work.
RETURN + RECOVER — assistant returns to primary zone; displaced work is recovered or remains visible backlog.

No invented numeric trigger. Existing plant safety/quality/process priorities and escalation rules govern.

Why this matters

Rigid zones can create local overload while the other operator has capacity. Uncontrolled helping creates duplicate response, extra walking and abandoned home-zone work. Controlled flex makes internal capacity sharing visible and testable.

A flex assist between Op1 and Op2 does not by itself make the period ASSISTED 2-OP because staffing remains two; it must be logged to evaluate zone balance and displaced-work risk.

Pilot integrity

INDEPENDENT 2-OP = frozen method + normal baseline support only. ASSISTED 2-OP = incremental third-person staffing substitution. CONTAINED = rescue/special cause means the concept is temporarily not being tested.

Independent Exposure % = Independent 2-op minutes / observable pilot minutes × 100
