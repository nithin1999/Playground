Latest improvement — Break-Relief Debt / Exported-Work Control

Qualified relief is not free capacity. If a Mold/Bladder operator, Team Lead, or another role covers Curing while their own required work continues to arrive, the apparent 2-operator gain can simply export workload and backlog to another role.

Capture every relief interval: relief person/role, coverage start/end, Curing work performed, home-role work due, work deferred, alternate coverage, recovery start/finish, recovery people-minutes and carryover.
Relief Debt: home-role required work that became deferred because the relief person was covering Curing. Track actual tasks first; do not invent time equivalents without observed or plant-standard work-element times.
Break feasibility: the Curing operator receives the intended coverage AND the relief mechanism creates no unmeasured labor, missed protected work or persistent backlog elsewhere.
Accounting: production assistance and recovery labor remain visible in Effective Labor Hours / Support Burden rather than disappearing behind a successful break handoff.
