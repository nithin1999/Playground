Latest improvement — Pilot Integrity Exposure States

Do not treat Thursday as one binary pass/fail shift. Timestamp the staffing condition actually being tested so hidden help cannot make a nominal 2-op condition look independent.

INDEPENDENT 2-OP: Op1 + Op2 run the frozen method with normal baseline support only.
ASSISTED 2-OP: incremental third-person coaching, fetching, coverage, prioritization or hands-on help occurs. Log role, reason, duration and people-minutes.
CONTAINED / NOT TESTABLE: safety/quality/process authority, major special cause or rescue temporarily means the frozen staffing concept is no longer being tested.

Independent Exposure % = Independent 2-op minutes / observable pilot minutes × 100

No acceptance percentage is invented before the pilot. Analyze why assisted/contained periods occur rather than discarding them.
