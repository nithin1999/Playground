Latest improvement — Demonstrated Skill-Coverage Gate

Do not let a time-balanced 2-op design pass if the actual pair cannot independently perform the critical work that may arrive. Replace a yes/no cross-training list with a Task × Person demonstrated skill matrix tied to the actual Op1, Op2, primary relief and alternate relief.

Track by task: required qualification; demonstrated status for Op1/Op2/relief; trainer/verifier; verification date/method; restrictions.
Readiness: Not trained → Training/practice → Demonstrated under observation → Approved independent where plant rules require approval. Training attendance alone is not independent capability.
Before exposure: run a Pairing Coverage Check against the people actually assigned that shift. Treat uncovered required skills as a staffing-design constraint, not a speed problem.
Close gaps: use plant-approved training and TWI Job Instruction/Job Breakdown Sheets for Important Steps, Key Points and Reasons Why.

No arbitrary minimum number of qualified people is imposed; plant requirements plus abnormal-response and relief rules determine required coverage.
