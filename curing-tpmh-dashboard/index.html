Decision logic

  • Good TPMH during low demand supports normal-state feasibility, not unobserved surge capability.
  • Naturally observed states need timestamps, operator load, Pending Work/recovery, response and support evidence.
  • Scenario walk-through = method verification, not pilot success.
  • Unencountered critical states remain NOT TESTED and become explicit residual risk.
  • Final recommendation reports both performance and exposure coverage.
