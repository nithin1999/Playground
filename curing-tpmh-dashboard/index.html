Latest improvement — Demand-Normalized Work Content Ledger

Do not judge two-operator feasibility from TPMH or occupancy alone. Separate observed operator work into fixed/periodic work and event-driven work, then relate event-driven labor to the actual exposure that generated it.

Work familyThursday captureDecision use
Fixed / periodicChecks, scans, scheduled rounds and other required recurring work: count × observed manual+walk minutesShows the minimum recurring labor load that exists even on a quiet shift.
Event-drivenAlarms, troubleshooting, manual load/unload, bladder/press response: event count × hands-on+walk minutesShows labor sensitivity to actual abnormal demand.
Motion / retrievalWalking, searching, fetching tools/material embedded in required workIdentifies recoverable capacity before adding headcount.
Support / reliefIncremental third-person and relief people-minutesPrevents exported work from making 2-op appear feasible.

Key rule: Thursday is one demand exposure. Record denominators such as running press-hours, production volume and relevant event counts so labor can be compared under different demand conditions. Do not extrapolate a single quiet or difficult shift to permanent staffing.

Thursday observation

Continuous: abnormalities, queue starts, flex assists, third-person help, exposure-state changes, break/relief transitions, delayed work, containment and recovery.

Fixed 15 min/hour: capture task start/end, operator, zone, category, walking/retrieval, machine interaction, zone adherence, overlapping demand, relief state and carryover work.

Hourly control: record plan/actual/cumulative gap plus downtime, abnormality state, staffing support, relief and protected work.

Capacity logic

Both committed + queue: investigate genuine demand/capacity collision.
One apparently available + queue: investigate skill, ownership, visibility, routing or priority failure.
Queue + incremental third person: quantify support people-minutes and recovery; do not hide staffing substitution.

Break-relief capacity

COVERED / DEGRADED / UNRELIEVED-CONTAINED. Keep relief debt visible until displaced Curing or relief-home-role work is recovered.

Pilot integrity

INDEPENDENT 2-OP = frozen method + normal baseline support. ASSISTED 2-OP = incremental staffing substitution. CONTAINED = concept temporarily not testable.

Independent Exposure % = Independent 2-op minutes / observable pilot minutes × 100
