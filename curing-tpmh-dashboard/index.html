Latest improvement — Primary/Flex Ownership + Handoff Trigger

Assign every press/zone and protected task a PRIMARY owner and a qualified FLEX backup. Flex does not automatically respond to every alarm. Transfer occurs when the primary is already committed, required work would otherwise wait, a two-person task is invoked, or plant containment requires support.

For each transfer capture: time | demand | primary status | flex trigger | transfer/assist | handback | consequence. This separates genuine two-operator capacity shortage from dispatch ambiguity, duplicated response and cross-traffic.

Why it matters

Repeated cross-zone flex calls indicate route/load imbalance. Repeated two-person overlap can reveal a structural coverage requirement. Duplicated response without a defined trigger is coordination/motion waste. Plant safety, quality and equipment-protection rules always override experimental ownership.
