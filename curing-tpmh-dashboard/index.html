Latest improvement — Pre-Exposure Readiness Gate + Change-Point Log

Do not begin a baseline or shadow window without documenting the actual starting condition. Otherwise staffing, equipment, backlog, relief or support differences can be mistaken for a 2-operator effect.

Before release: verify actual Op1/Op2/shadow/leader/relief + qualifications, active press/gantry state, open safety/quality restrictions, starting backlog/protected work, break relief, zone/route standard, abnormal escalation method and observer/data readiness.
Release state: READY / CONDITIONALLY READY / HOLD. Existing plant safety, quality and authorization requirements govern blocking conditions; no invented numerical threshold.
During exposure: timestamp operator substitutions, relief changes, press/gantry state changes, unusual maintenance/manual support, mix changes, zone reassignment and major backlog transitions.
Analysis rule: compare stable-condition segments; do not hide material change points inside one shift average.
