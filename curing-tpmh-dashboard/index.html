Decision logic

One operator overloaded while the other has recoverable capacity = rebalance zones/routes/ownership first. Both occupied while queues and Recovery Debt grow = stronger capacity/surge evidence. Overload only during breaks = relief constraint. Walking/retrieval driving imbalance = motion/layout target. No arbitrary utilization threshold is invented.
