Latest improvement — Demand-Weighted Operator Balance

Do not lock Op1/Op2 as an equal press-count split. Balance the two zones on observed work content + travel + abnormal exposure + relief/recovery demand.

Yamazumi by zone/window: stack recurring manual work, travel, abnormal hands-on work, relief and recovery against available operator minutes.
Machine-intensive rule: use operator-demand minutes rather than machine cycle time as the staffing denominator; inspect peaks and concurrency as well as averages.
No false precision: alarm duration is not labor. Until hands-on response is measured, keep abnormal labor as an explicit unknown/sensitivity term.
Rebalance: zone boundaries are provisional. Move presses/work elements if observed Op1/Op2 demand is persistently unequal.
