Latest improvement — Qualification-Gated Relief

A named relief person is not usable capacity until the work they may inherit is verified within plant qualifications. Build a compact coverage matrix: critical task or zone by Op1, Op2, planned relief and leader/flex. Mark only VERIFIED, NOT VERIFIED or UNKNOWN.

Break test: before each planned break, verify that the remaining operator plus relief can cover the work that may arise. If a critical task has no verified alternate, treat that interval as a known coverage gap and follow existing plant relief and escalation rules.

During the pilot, log work that waits specifically because the available person is not qualified. This separates labor-capacity shortage from a cross-training or coverage constraint.

Base Load vs Surge Load

Build observed work-combination views for each operator: manual, walk/retrieval, machine-auto, protected/periodic and abnormal/event. Low average utilization alone does not prove removable labor.
