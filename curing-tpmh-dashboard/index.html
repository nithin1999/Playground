Flag single-point skills, relief gaps, two-person tasks, and legitimate normal support dependencies. For every queued event, ask whether an available operator was actually qualified to own it.

Capacity diagnosis

Both qualified operators committed + queue: evidence of a physical capacity collision.
Operator physically free but not qualified + queue: skill-constrained capacity; investigate cross-training/authorization.
Qualified operator free + queue: investigate ownership, visibility, routing, priority or measurement error.
Repeated third-person help: distinguish normal structural support from incremental 2-op staffing substitution.

Demand-normalized work content

Separate fixed/periodic work, event-driven work, motion/retrieval and support/relief. Retain running press-hours, production volume and relevant event counts so one quiet or difficult shift is not generalized into permanent staffing.

Thursday observation

Continuous: abnormalities, queues, flex assists, third-person help, exposure-state changes, break/relief transitions, delayed work, containment and recovery.

Fixed 15 min/hour: task start/end, operator, zone, category, walking/retrieval, machine interaction, zone adherence, overlapping demand, relief state and carryover work.

Skill field for abnormal work: required skill, primary operator qualification, flex qualification, relief qualification, and whether a skill gap contributed to waiting.

Break-relief capacity

COVERED / DEGRADED / UNRELIEVED-CONTAINED. A relief person is not full coverage unless required critical skills are actually covered. Keep relief debt visible until displaced work is recovered.

Pilot integrity

INDEPENDENT 2-OP = frozen method + normal baseline support. ASSISTED 2-OP = incremental staffing substitution. CONTAINED = concept temporarily not testable.

Independent Exposure % = Independent 2-op minutes / observable pilot minutes × 100
