Latest improvement — Demand-State Exposure Coverage

A good 12-hour average is not enough. For each 15-minute window, retain running-press exposure, abnormal/event starts, protected/manual work state, and break/relief state, then pair them with saturation, recovery debt, support and output.

After the pilot build an Exposure Coverage Matrix. Use observed distributions or historical quantiles to distinguish low, typical and higher exposure rather than inventing thresholds beforehand. Any important state not experienced Thursday is marked NOT TESTED.

Why it matters

A clean low-demand shift demonstrates feasibility only for that exposure. Stronger evidence requires representative and higher-demand states without persistent saturation, protected-work lateness, unrecovered debt or staffing substitution. Missing exposure should trigger another controlled test, not extrapolation.

Controls retained

Two-operator saturation logging; qualification-gated relief; Base vs Surge load; protected-work due-time control; Recovery Debt; containment rules; leader/flex support displacement; standard-work conformance; matched-condition comparison; early/mid/late/break analysis.

Best current strategy

  1. Freeze people, zones, routes, qualifications, relief and reaction rules.
  2. Verify qualification-gated coverage and protected-work ownership.
  3. Scenario-walk breaks, simultaneous demand, two-person tasks and containment/restart.
  4. Run Thursday with timestamped exceptions, 15-minute workload, hourly plan-vs-actual, queues and saturation episodes.
  5. Capture exposure context in every 15-minute window.
  6. Build observed work-combination views and separate BASE from SURGE load.
  7. Reconstruct recovery debt, support displacement, qualification waits, saturation and deviations.
  8. Build the Demand-State Exposure Coverage Matrix and mark missing states NOT TESTED.
  9. Normalize for press-hours, events, mix and downtime.
  10. Fix only the demonstrated constraint and repeat missing/representative exposure before permanent staffing change.

Immediate next action

Add four fields to the 15-minute log: running presses/press-hours | abnormal/event starts | protected/manual work active | break/relief state.
