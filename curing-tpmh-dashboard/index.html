Latest improvement — Process Confirmation + Adherence Layer

A pilot outcome is ambiguous if we cannot tell whether the proposed 2-operator standard was actually executed. Add lightweight process-confirmation sampling during baseline, scenario challenge and shadow exposure.

Confirm critical behaviors: assigned ownership/route, one primary abnormality owner, protected due work handled/queued, support logged, and explicit break handoff.
Record by opportunity: Standard followed Y/N → deviation reason → consequence → countermeasure owner.
Separate causes: design failure, training/clarity gap, abnormal/special cause, or non-adherence. Repeated inability to execute a clear standard is itself design evidence.
No invented pass rate: collect observed adherence and consequences first; safety/quality requirements remain mandatory.
