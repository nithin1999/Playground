Latest improvement — Event-Driven Dispatch Priority Ladder

PRIMARY/FLEX ownership tells us who owns work; the new control tells operators what to do first when several valid demands compete. Use one plant-approved ladder: plant safety/quality/equipment containment first; then plant-defined time-critical abnormal response; then protected/periodic work according to its real due requirement; then normal owned route work; then discretionary noncritical work. Existing plant rules always override this framework.

For each meaningful reprioritization capture: time | competing demands | selected demand | governing priority/reason | deferred work | FLEX invoked? | recovery time | consequence. Do not invent response-time or lateness limits.

Why it matters

In event-driven machine-intensive work, unclear dispatch can create task switching, excess walking, duplicate response and silent deferral even when two-person labor content is feasible. The pilot must distinguish true saturation from a sequencing/dispatch problem.
