Future-state standard-work package

  1. Operator route charts: zones, home positions, routine routes, press locations, crossover/support paths, point-of-use materials, safety/quality checkpoints.
  2. Time-phased workload: separate manual work, necessary walking and machine processing; retain the 30-minute Green/Yellow/Red demand profile.
  3. Abnormal-response sheet: alarm ownership, cross-support, qualified-backup call and supervision escalation.
  4. Backup Job Instruction: task-level major steps/key points plus demonstrated qualification for Mold, Bladder and Team Lead relief.
  5. Pilot intervention log: every third-operator intervention tied back to the proposed standard.

Design principle

Standardize predictable work
GIP/material routes, checks, documentation, home positions and break coverage can have explicit standard methods.
Keep abnormalities signal-driven
Do not force alarm response into a fake repetitive patrol. Alarm/andon signal determines destination; the response rule determines ownership/support.
No invented thresholds
Leave response-time and backup-trigger limits TBD until shadow-pilot evidence supports them.

Best current strategy

  1. Map press layout and make alarm source visible.
  2. Balance Op 1 / Op 2 by occupied workload, peaks and travel.
  3. Create the two-operator standardized-work package.
  4. Qualify backup by task, not job title.
  5. Use point-of-use staging to reduce routine travel.
  6. Protect Mold/Bladder due work and carryover.
  7. Baseline → shadow 2-op → revise standard → sustained rotation → true 2-op.
