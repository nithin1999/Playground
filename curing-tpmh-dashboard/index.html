Latest improvement: Effective Manning / Support-Labor Accounting

A nominal two-operator shift can be propped up by break relief, leaders, mold/bladder operators, maintenance or other helpers. Track both local headcount and total labor consumed by the operating model.

NORMAL SHARED SUPPORTPILOT-ONLY HELPEMERGENCY / SAFETY

Decision rule

Report Core 2-Operator Hours separately from External Support / Relief Hours. If another role performs work that would otherwise belong to the two operators, timestamp it and expose the labor. If a borrowed person creates backlog in the donor role, record that recovery too. Do not invent an allowable support threshold.

Why this matters

Break relief can be a legitimate future-state design if it is an approved shared system, but it is not free labor. Pilot-only assistance cannot be treated as proof that a standalone two-operator model is sustainable. Borrowing mold/bladder or other personnel can transfer workload rather than eliminate it.
