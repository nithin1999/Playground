Latest improvement — 30-minute Capacity Reserve Profile

Do not approve two operators from average utilization or shift-end TPMH. For each 30-minute window compare qualified operator-minutes actually available with demanded operator-minutes.

Demand: protected routine work due + required travel + measured hands-on abnormalities + unresolved/deferred work carried into the interval.
Available capacity: qualified operator-minutes actually available after breaks and other required duties. A relief helper contributes only the capacity truly available after the helper's own due work.
Reserve: available qualified operator-minutes − demanded operator-minutes. Mark qualification conflicts, simultaneous-response periods, outside support, delayed relief, backlog and escalation on the same timeline.

Compare the identical profile for the current 3-op baseline and shadow 2-op. Do not invent a reserve/utilization threshold; establish the baseline distribution and honor existing plant safety/quality/process requirements.

Best current strategy

  1. Map travel; remove avoidable motion; balance zones on workload + peaks + travel.
  2. Freeze NORMAL-mode routes for predictable work.
  3. Freeze ABNORMAL-mode dispatch, cross-support, escalation and return-to-standard rules.
  4. Make interrupted routine work visible in a pending-work/recovery queue.
  5. Verify two-deep critical-task skills.
  6. Reserve real break-relief capacity.
  7. Build 30-minute Capacity Reserve Profiles for normal, abnormal and break windows.
  8. Freeze measurement, Leader Standard Work, SIC and escalation authority.
  9. Collect context-characterized 3-op baseline with identical measures.
  10. Shadow 2-op; log support, backlog, relief, qualification, route and escalation exceptions.
  11. Pareto low-reserve/exception windows → revise → sustained A/B/C/D rotation → true 2-op only after gates pass.
