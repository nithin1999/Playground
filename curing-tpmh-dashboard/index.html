Latest improvement - Protected-Work Backlog Aging

Alarm response alone cannot prove 2-operator capacity. Thursday must show whether required recurring work accumulates and whether the system can recover it independently.

Best current strategy

  1. Freeze people, zones, normal routes, qualifications, relief and reaction rules.
  2. List protected/periodic tasks with actual due-time requirements.
  3. Scenario-walk simultaneous demand, break abnormality, flex assist and two-person tasks.
  4. Rehearse interruption recovery and backlog visibility.
  5. Run Thursday with exception logging, fixed observation windows and hourly production/backlog control.
  6. Reconstruct occupancy, queues, interruptions, backlog aging/recovery and support.
  7. Normalize against running presses, events, mix and downtime.
  8. Compare early, middle and late shift robustness and backlog behavior.
  9. Review TPMH, Effective Labor, Independent Exposure, support, relief debt and protected-work completion.
  10. Pareto observed causes and repeat representative conditions before permanent staffing change.

Immediate next action

Create the protected-work backlog ledger from the real recurring-task list before Thursday. For each item retain task, zone, due time, pending-since time, owner, displacement reason, start, completion and helper. Rehearse one alarm + break scenario and confirm the backlog can be followed from creation through recovery.
