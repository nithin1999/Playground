Latest improvement — Pilot Decision Scorecard

Freeze a multi-metric scorecard before the first 2-op exposure. Do not judge feasibility from TPMH or output alone; a labor-productivity gain can otherwise hide delayed work, borrowed labor, relief failure or downstream carryover.

Guardrails: existing plant safety/quality requirements and required task completion.
Delivery/process: plan vs actual, running press-hours/cycles, abnormal-response and downtime context.
Labor integrity: Curing people-hours, TPMH, outside-support people-minutes, relief support and overtime where applicable.
Standard-work health: backlog/age, route exceptions, qualification conflicts, relief exceptions and escalations.
Support-system health: Mold/Bladder due/completed/carryover and helper work displaced by Curing support.

Use existing plant requirements where limits already exist. For measures without established limits, first collect the identical 3-op baseline; do not invent pass/fail thresholds.

Best current strategy

  1. Map travel; remove avoidable motion; balance zones on workload + peaks + travel.
  2. Freeze NORMAL routes and ABNORMAL dispatch/recovery rules.
  3. Protect interrupted routine work with a visible recovery queue.
  4. Verify two-deep skills and reserve real break-relief capacity.
  5. Build 30-minute Capacity Reserve + Recovery Profiles.
  6. Freeze measurement, Leader Standard Work, SIC and escalation authority.
  7. Freeze Pilot Context Card + Pilot Decision Scorecard.
  8. Collect context-characterized 3-op baseline using identical definitions.
  9. Shadow 2-op and compare with context-matched 3-op periods on the full outcome vector.
  10. Pareto structural-overload/support/backlog/relief exceptions and revise.
  11. Expand across underrepresented contexts and A/B/C/D crews; true 2-op only after representative gates pass.
