Why it matters

  • Queue count alone can hide delayed protected work or incomplete recovery.
  • A stable queue with increasing oldest-item age means the system may not actually be recovering.
  • Waiting while one operator is free but unqualified points toward skill coverage/dispatch rather than raw labor.
  • Waiting while both qualified operators are legitimately committed is stronger evidence of a local capacity/concurrency constraint.
  • If old work clears only after outside help, the pilot is support-dependent.
  • No arbitrary service threshold is introduced; use verified plant requirements and mark unknowns NOT VERIFIED.
