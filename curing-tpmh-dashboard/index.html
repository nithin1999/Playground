Latest improvement — two-mode dispatch standard

A rigid repeating cycle is not a good representation of event-driven Curing work. Freeze two linked operating modes instead.

NORMAL: Op1 and Op2 follow workload/travel-balanced geographic routes for predictable GIP/scan, checks, monitoring and protected routine work.
ABNORMAL: an alarm or defined abnormality activates the dispatch ladder: zone owner → qualified cross-support → Green/Amber/Red escalation when required. Interrupted routine work becomes visible pending work with owner/recovery status.

After control is restored, check the pending-work queue and recover protected work in plant-required priority order before treating the system as back to normal. Do not invent numeric queue/WIP or response limits; use existing plant requirements and later baseline/shadow evidence.

Best current strategy

  1. Map travel; remove avoidable motion; balance zones on workload + peaks + travel.
  2. Freeze NORMAL-mode routes for predictable work.
  3. Freeze ABNORMAL-mode dispatch, cross-support, escalation and return-to-standard rules.
  4. Make interrupted routine work visible in a pending-work/recovery queue.
  5. Verify two-deep critical-task skills.
  6. Reserve real break-relief capacity.
  7. Prove 30-minute capacity for normal + abnormal + break windows.
  8. Freeze measurement, Leader Standard Work, SIC and escalation authority.
  9. Collect context-characterized 3-op baseline with identical measures.
  10. Shadow 2-op; log support, backlog, relief, route and escalation exceptions.
  11. Pareto causes → revise → sustained A/B/C/D rotation → true 2-op only after gates pass.
