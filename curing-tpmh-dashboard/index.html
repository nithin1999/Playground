Latest improvement — Exception-Based Pilot Observation

Use two observation layers so the pilot captures both normal-state evidence and the exceptions that determine whether two operators are sustainable.

Event-triggered: log every third-person intervention, simultaneous abnormality, delayed protected task, relief problem, cross-zone assist, skill constraint, avoidable motion, escalation, and backlog creation/recovery. Capture trigger, state, response, support people-minutes and recovery.
Periodic confirmation: at a practical cadence frozen before the exposure, snapshot Op1/Op2 location, NORMAL/ABNORMAL mode, pending-work queue, relief state and whether the defined standard is usable.
Deviation rule: do not score operator compliance. Record why the standard could not be followed: equipment, layout/motion, skill, material, competing task, unclear standard, or other. Use this for kaizen.

No universal audit frequency or intervention limit is invented. Consequential exceptions are captured when they occur; periodic cadence is selected before the pilot based on observer feasibility.
