Latest improvement — Effective Labor / Support Burden Test

A 2-operator exposure must be judged on total labor consumed by the system, not only the two scheduled Curing operators.

Effective labor hours = direct Curing labor hours + staffing-dependent outside-support people-minutes / 60.
Support burden = staffing-dependent outside-support people-minutes / exposure hours.
Adjusted TPMH = tires produced / effective labor hours. Report scheduled-headcount TPMH beside it so hidden labor is visible.

Use the same intervention taxonomy in the 3-op baseline and 2-op exposure. Separate normal supervisory/process support from work that substitutes for removed Curing capacity. Count break relief and cross-support explicitly. No arbitrary acceptable support threshold is invented; establish normal support from baseline evidence first.

Best current strategy

  2. Freeze NORMAL routes and ABNORMAL dispatch/recovery rules.
  3. Protect interrupted routine work with a visible recovery queue.
  4. Build demonstrated skill coverage for the actual Op1/Op2/relief pairing; close gaps with TWI/plant-approved training.
  5. Reserve real break-relief capacity.
  6. Build 30-minute Capacity Reserve + Recovery Profiles with qualification constraints.
  7. Freeze measurement, Leader Standard Work, SIC and escalation authority.
  8. Freeze Context Card + Decision Scorecard + Exception Observation + support intervention taxonomy.
  9. Collect context-characterized 3-op baseline including normal outside-support labor.
  10. Shadow 2-op; capture exceptions and support people-minutes; compare with context-matched 3-op periods.
  11. Calculate scheduled TPMH, Effective Labor Hours, Support Burden and Adjusted TPMH; Pareto incremental support and overload.
  12. Revise and expand across underrepresented contexts and A/B/C/D crews; true 2-op only after representative gates pass without hidden labor transfer.
