Latest improvement — Pilot Leader Standard Work / Adherence Control

A 2-op exposure needs an authoritative leader role that verifies the operating standard without becoming hidden third-operator labor. This separates standard adherence from system feasibility.

Before exposure: confirm pair, verified qualifications, zones, break relief and open abnormalities.
During exposure: verify NORMAL/ABNORMAL ownership and queue state; reinforce the standard, remove barriers and record deviations/reasons.
Protect the experiment: any leader production/operator work is outside-support people-minutes; coaching/verification remains leader work.
Classify evidence: standard not followed/ownership unclear; standard followed but capacity/skill/response failed; external/special cause; or not yet classifiable.
Close exposure: review backlog, support labor, breaks, containment and unresolved deviations before judging feasibility.

Best current strategy

  1. Remove avoidable motion and point-of-use gaps.
  2. Build Demand-Weighted Operator Balance; define provisional zones by work content + travel + abnormal exposure.
  3. Build fixed-window Variability-Aware Reserve distributions for Op1, Op2 and the pair.
  4. Build Cross-Zone Collision Matrix and test alternate zone boundaries.
  5. Apply Qualification Redundancy / Pair-Robustness Gate so reserve is qualification-constrained.
  6. Freeze NORMAL routes and ABNORMAL single-owner dispatch.
  7. Protect due work with the Protected Work Matrix and visible recovery queue.
  8. Close critical single-point skill gaps with standardized training and demonstrated qualification.
  9. Use formal break handoffs and retain relief person's home-role demand.
  10. Add Pilot Leader Standard Work to verify adherence and control escalation without hidden operator substitution.
  11. Freeze measurement, escalation and containment/restart rules.
  12. Collect context-characterized 3-op baseline with reserve + collision + skill + adherence evidence.
  13. Shadow 2-op; measure queue aging, support labor, breaks, containment, collision handling and adherence.
  14. Calculate scheduled TPMH, Effective Labor and Adjusted TPMH.
  15. Pareto exceptions; rebalance work/zone boundaries, training and standard-work gaps.
  16. Expand across demand, downtime, late-shift and A/B/C/D conditions before true 2-op.
