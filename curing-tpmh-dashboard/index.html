Latest improvement — Break-Relief Coverage Debt

A sustainable two-operator design must prove that scheduled breaks do not simply transfer Curing work to another role or postpone required work until the operator returns.

For each break capture start/end, operator off route, qualified coverage source, relief home role, work accepted, work deferred, oldest deferred age, support people-minutes, donor work displaced, backlog at return, and time normal route is restored.

No numeric pass threshold is invented. A clean exposure requires planned breaks, qualified coverage, controlled required work, and recovery of both Curing and the donor role without hidden labor.

Interpretation

Debt only during breaks points to relief design. Donor-role backlog indicates staffing substitution. Missed or shortened breaks invalidate the exposure as evidence of sustainable 12-hour staffing. Debt persisting after return indicates a recovery-capacity issue.

Best current strategy

  1. Freeze people, zones, routes, qualifications, relief and plant reaction rules.
  2. Assign PRIMARY/FLEX ownership and dispatch priority.
  3. Verify qualification-gated relief and protected-work due requirements.
  4. Scenario-walk every break, competing demand and abnormal condition.
  5. Run Thursday with timestamped workload, queues, saturation and support.
  6. Reconcile each break for Curing backlog and donor-role displacement.
  7. Build Base versus Surge work-combination views.
  8. Reconstruct Recovery Debt and Break-Relief Coverage Debt.
  9. Normalize for press-hours, events, mix and downtime.
  10. Fix only the demonstrated constraint and repeat representative exposure.
