The initial qualified owner remains responsible until close or explicit handoff. The second operator continues protected/base work unless an existing plant rule, qualification need, required two-person task, or authorized escalation requires assistance.

Why it matters

  • Unnecessary two-person convergence can create apparent saturation without an aggregate manpower shortage.
  • If the second operator stays on base work and required demand still waits, evidence for a true capacity or skill constraint becomes stronger.
  • Frequent qualification-driven handoffs point toward cross-training rather than simply adding labor.
  • Required two-person and authorized containment work stays separate and is never classified as waste.
